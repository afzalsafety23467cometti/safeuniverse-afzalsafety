Why Safety Comes First

Workplace safety, as a formal practice, grew out of the Industrial Revolution — a time when factories, mines and construction sites saw a sharp rise in accidents and worker injuries. As industries grew, so did the need to protect the people running them. Over the 20th century this awareness evolved into structured Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) systems, backed by laws and standards such as OSHA, the UK's HSE, and international frameworks like ISO 45001 — turning safety from an afterthought into a core part of how every serious organization operates.

Today, HSE matters because it protects the most valuable asset on any site: people. A strong safety culture reduces injuries and fatalities, keeps operations legally compliant, prevents costly downtime, and builds trust between workers and management. Safe Universe HSE exists to make that knowledge simple, searchable and practical — so every worker, supervisor and safety officer can find the hazards and controls they need, when they need them.
